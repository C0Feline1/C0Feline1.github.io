Privacy Policy

Stardream Gallery (星梦画廊) · Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how Stardream Gallery, package com.stardreamgallery.game, handles information when you use the game. It applies to the Android game and its in-game advertising features.

Information stored by the game

The game does not require an account. Game progress, puzzle and chapter progress, favorites, reward history, and game preferences are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. The developer does not receive this saved game data, and it is not synchronized to a developer-operated server. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The game includes Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded ads (for example, to receive Star Sand or skip a level) and occasional full-screen interstitial ads (for example, between levels or after several back navigations). The ads SDK may initialize when the Android game starts, and ad requests are made to load and show these ads. Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share with Google information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad requests, and video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, crashes or hangs, and energy use; and
  • device or account identifiers, which may include the advertising ID or App Set ID, subject to SDK settings, Android controls, and applicable restrictions.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain their services. The game limits ad content to a maximum PG rating. This setting does not mean that the advertising SDK collects no information. Google describes its data practices in the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and its Privacy Policy.

In regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time from "Ad Privacy" in the game's settings.

The game does not request precise location, camera, microphone, or contacts access. Google may infer general location from an IP address as described above.

Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. The game does not send locally stored game progress to the developer. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads is shared with Google and, as needed to provide advertising services, relevant advertising partners under their own terms and privacy practices.

Security and retention

Game progress remains in the app's local storage until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Google states that Google Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit using TLS; Google determines retention for information handled by its services under its own policies. The developer does not maintain a server-side player profile or copy of game progress.

Age requirement

Stardream Gallery is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help address it.

Your choices

You can choose whether to start an offered rewarded ad. Where consent applies, you can change your ad consent choice from "Ad Privacy" in the game's settings. You can reset locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. You can also manage advertising and privacy controls available in Android and Google settings. For information about Google's ad and privacy controls, see Google My Ad Center.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Stardream Gallery at chuyifan428@gmail.com.

隐私权政策

星梦画廊（Stardream Gallery）· 生效日期：2026年10月15日

本政策说明你使用星梦画廊 Android 游戏（包名 com.stardreamgallery.game）及其游戏内广告功能时，信息如何被处理。

游戏本地保存的信息

游戏无需创建账号。游戏进度、拼图和章节进度、收藏、奖励记录及游戏偏好保存在设备本地的应用存储中。开发者不会收到这些存档，游戏也不会将其同步到开发者运营的服务器。你可以通过清除应用存储数据或卸载游戏来删除这些本地数据。

广告与 Google Mobile Ads

游戏集成 Google Mobile Ads，用于可选的激励广告（例如领取星砂或跳过关卡），以及偶尔出现的全屏插页广告（例如关卡之间或多次返回操作后）。Android 游戏启动时广告 SDK 可能会初始化，游戏会发送广告请求以加载和展示这些广告。Google Mobile Ads 可能自动收集并与 Google 共享以下信息：

  • IP 地址，可能用于估算设备的大致位置；
  • 应用和广告互动信息，例如应用启动、点击、广告请求和视频观看；
  • 诊断和性能信息，例如启动时间、崩溃或卡顿情况及能耗；
  • 设备或账号标识符，可能包括广告 ID 或 App Set ID，具体受 SDK 设置、Android 控制和适用限制影响。

Google 及其广告合作方可能使用这些信息来投放和衡量广告、防止欺诈并维护服务。游戏将广告内容评级上限设置为 PG 级。该设置不代表广告 SDK 完全不收集信息。Google 在Google Mobile Ads 数据披露说明和Google 隐私权政策中介绍了其数据处理方式。

在法律要求征得同意的地区（例如欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士），游戏会通过 Google 用户消息平台（UMP）在投放个性化广告前征求你的同意。你可以随时在游戏设置的“广告隐私设置”中查看或更改你的选择。

游戏不请求精确位置信息、相机、麦克风或通讯录权限。Google 可能如上所述根据 IP 地址推断大致位置。

信息共享

开发者不会出售个人信息。游戏不会将本地存档发送给开发者。Google Mobile Ads 处理的信息会与 Google 共享，并可能按提供广告服务所需与相关广告合作方共享；这些服务适用各自的条款和隐私政策。

安全与保留期限

本地游戏进度会保留在应用存储中，直到你清除应用数据或卸载游戏。Google 表示 Google Mobile Ads 数据在传输过程中使用 TLS 加密；由 Google 服务处理的信息由 Google 按其政策决定保留期限。开发者不维护服务器端玩家档案，也不保存游戏进度副本。

年龄要求

星梦画廊面向 13 岁及以上玩家，并非针对 13 岁以下儿童设计。我们不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如家长或监护人认为儿童提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会协助处理。

你的选择

你可以选择是否观看游戏中提供的激励广告。在适用同意要求的地区，你可以在游戏设置的“广告隐私设置”中更改广告同意选择。清除应用存储数据或卸载游戏可重置本地存档。你也可以使用 Android 和 Google 设置中提供的广告与隐私控制。Google 的广告和隐私控制信息见 Google 我的广告中心。

政策更新

如果游戏或数据处理方式发生变化，我们可能会更新本政策。更新时会修改页面顶部的生效日期。
